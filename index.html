Dear My Powerpuff Girls ❤️

Some friendships happen by chance. Ours happened in school... and somehow became one of the best parts of my life. From classrooms... to college stories... to careers... to random gossip... to planning trips... to wine nights... to becoming each other's biggest cheerleaders... we've literally grown up together.

We've laughed until our stomachs hurt. Taken thousands of photos. Travelled together. Made memories we'll tell our kids someday.

And I honestly hope this never changes. Let's keep travelling. Let's keep celebrating birthdays. Let's keep collecting passport stamps. Let's keep drinking wine together. Let's grow old together while still behaving like we're sixteen.

No matter where life takes us... I'll always be grateful that I got my own little Powerpuff Girls. Love you forever. ❤️ Happy Friendship Day!
